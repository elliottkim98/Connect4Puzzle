Part 5 Question 5
heuristic 2 (the new heuristic)
-----------
This heuristic returns a priority number, where each current tiles are interpreted and the numbers of row apart and columns apart from the goal tiles are added to prior. The more less the numbers, current tiles, are apart, the smaller the return value would be.
